Curiosity

FORMA is an independent space for making, testing, and sharing digital experiences. A practice built around the belief that the best way to understand an idea is to interact with it.

Structure / 001Form is never static

01 / The point of view

Digital work should do more than look good. It should make you feel, think, and participate.

What guides the work.

02 / Working principles

01 — EXPERIENCE

Make it tangible.

Give ideas a surface, a response, and a reason to be explored. Interaction is part of the message.

02 — CURIOSITY

Stay in motion.

Experimentation is not a detour. It is how new directions emerge and better questions get asked.

03 — CLARITY

Keep the signal.

Every detail should earn its place. Visual character and usability should strengthen one another.

04 — CRAFT

Care about the edges.

Responsive behavior, accessibility, performance, and small interaction details are part of the design.

05 — PLAY

Leave room to discover.

Not everything needs to be predictable. Thoughtful surprises make digital spaces memorable.

06 — PURPOSE

Build with direction.

Technology is a medium, not the destination. Start with the experience and choose tools to serve it.

03 / The practice

Independent
by nature.

FORMA is being shaped as a personal creative-technology flagship and an evolving independent product. It brings together interface design, creative coding, motion, and 3D exploration under one coherent point of view. The work is intentionally iterative: prototypes are made visible, tested, refined, and only then turned into product.

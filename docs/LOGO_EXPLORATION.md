# FORMA — Logo Exploration

**Phase:** 04 — Visual Design System  
**Status:** Exploratory concepts; no mark selected or approved  
**Purpose:** Translate the approved logo direction into three testable, original, flat-vector routes.

## Design constraints

- The wordmark must read clearly as FORMA at small and large sizes.
- The symbol must work independently at favicon/app-icon scale.
- All concepts must work in one color on both light and dark surfaces.
- No gradients, glow, bevels, 3D effects, or fine detail that disappears at 16–32 px.
- The symbol should relate to structure, form, transformation, or dimensional thinking.
- These are exploratory vector studies, not final trademark-cleared artwork.

## Route A — Modular F

**Idea:** A compact F built from a strong vertical spine and two offset horizontal planes. It is the most direct and recognizable route.

**Strengths**
- Clear relationship to the FORMA name.
- Strong silhouette at small sizes.
- Easy to reproduce in one color and use as a favicon.

**Risks**
- A modular F can feel familiar or generic unless proportions and negative space are distinctive.
- Needs optical refinement at tiny sizes.

**Best role:** Primary candidate for a product mark that needs immediate recognition.

## Route B — Form Shift

**Idea:** Two simple geometric outlines suggest a shape changing from a flat plane into a more dimensional form. The gap between them represents transformation.

**Strengths**
- Expresses experimentation and transformation rather than only the initial letter.
- More ownable conceptual territory if simplified carefully.
- Connects to the product's creative playground identity.

**Risks**
- Can become abstract or ambiguous at small sizes.
- Needs a disciplined silhouette to avoid looking like a generic cube or wireframe.

**Best role:** A more expressive secondary symbol if the geometry remains legible.

## Route C — Structural Frame

**Idea:** An open, precise frame with a deliberate interruption. It suggests a workspace, boundary, and the act of shaping something within a system.

**Strengths**
- Minimal and architectural.
- Fits the editorial precision direction.
- Works naturally as a container or framing device in the wider visual identity.

**Risks**
- Less directly connected to the name.
- Could be mistaken for a generic crop/frame icon without a distinctive proportion.

**Best role:** A supporting graphic motif or alternate symbol, rather than the strongest primary mark.

## Recommendation

Advance **Route A — Modular F** as the lead candidate, while keeping Route B as the challenger. Route C is useful as a supporting graphic language.

This is a recommendation, not an approval. The final mark should be selected only after checking:
1. Recognition at 16, 24, and 32 px.
2. Wordmark balance beside the symbol.
3. Positive and reversed monochrome versions.
4. Favicon and app-icon crop.
5. Distinctiveness against common lettermark and geometric-logo patterns.

## Wordmark direction

- Uppercase FORMA.
- Space Grotesk Semibold as the initial typographic reference.
- Custom spacing and optical alignment should be reviewed before outlining letters.
- Keep the wordmark flat and monochrome by default.
- Do not force a symbol into the letter O unless it improves readability and distinctiveness.

## Review decision

Product owner to choose a lead route, request refinements, or reject all three. No production logo, app icon, or brand lockup should be treated as final until this decision is made.

# FORMA — Hybrid Identity System

**Phase:** 04 — Visual Design System  
**Status:** Refined proposal; pending product-owner approval  
**Direction:** Modular Frame — a synthesis of Modular F, Form Shift, and Structural Frame

## 1. Design rationale

The product owner chose to combine the three exploratory routes. This proposal gives each route a distinct job rather than stacking three ideas into one complicated emblem:

- **Modular F** provides the recognizable core silhouette and connection to the FORMA name.
- **Structural Frame** informs the open geometry, alignment, and disciplined negative space.
- **Form Shift** becomes a supporting graphic and motion principle: offset repetitions can show a form changing state.

The primary symbol therefore remains simple. Transformation belongs in supporting compositions and motion, not in tiny details inside the core mark.

## 2. Proposed primary mark — Modular Frame

A custom geometric F built from a strong vertical spine and two horizontal arms. A restrained open frame/offset corner gives the mark a sense of structure and dimensional progression without turning it into a cube or wireframe.

### Construction principles
- One-color silhouette first.
- Square geometry with optical spacing, not arbitrary decorative cuts.
- Strong vertical spine; shorter middle arm; longer top arm.
- Keep the lower-right quadrant open to preserve the frame idea and avoid a closed-box appearance.
- Avoid thin outlines that disappear at small sizes.
- Use the symbol without the wordmark at compact sizes.

## 3. Wordmark

- Uppercase **FORMA**.
- Space Grotesk Semibold is the typographic starting point; final production artwork should be optically reviewed and may require custom letter spacing or outlined paths.
- Use a restrained, tight but readable tracking.
- Keep the symbol and wordmark aligned by visual center, not merely bounding-box center.
- The wordmark is not to be distorted, skewed, or decorated with multiple accent colors.

The current SVG uses a font-family reference and remains editable. It is not yet a font-outlined production master.

## 4. Color behavior

The logo is monochrome by default:
- Obsidian on Soft Canvas.
- Soft Canvas on Obsidian.
- Single-color black/white reproduction when brand colors are unavailable.

Electric Lime may appear as a supporting brand field or small interface accent, but the primary logo should not require lime to remain recognizable. Coral and Digital Blue are not primary logo colors.

## 5. Supporting graphic language

Use the Form Shift idea as a graphic device:
- Repeated outlines or solid planes with a small, deliberate offset.
- Before/after states.
- Cropped structural frames.
- Simple diagrams showing a transformation from one state to another.

Do not use these motifs as permanent decoration behind every heading or card. They should explain or reinforce a concept.

## 6. Responsive logo set

The proposed asset set includes:
- Primary horizontal lockup for navigation and documentation.
- Standalone symbol for compact navigation and avatars.
- Monochrome reversed lockup for dark surfaces.
- Favicon artwork with simplified geometry and no wordmark.
- App-icon composition with generous safe area.

At 16 px, use the simplified symbol, not the full horizontal lockup. Check legibility at 16, 24, 32, and 48 px.

## 7. Clear space and minimum use

- Maintain clear space around the lockup of at least the width of the symbol's vertical spine.
- Do not place text, borders, or other marks inside this clear space.
- Do not use the full lockup below the size at which FORMA remains comfortably readable.
- Do not add shadows, gradients, bevels, glow, or 3D treatments.
- Do not stretch, rotate, outline, or rearrange the symbol's parts.

## 8. Motion behavior

If animated, the symbol may transition between:
1. A stable structural frame.
2. The modular F becoming visually apparent.
3. A settled, still final mark.

Motion should be short, purposeful, and optional. It must not loop continuously in the global navigation. Reduced-motion mode displays the static mark immediately.
